# Playbook: demo day (3 minutes, live product)

**Preconditions**: a working prototype, three minutes, a jury or audience that decides in the room. Event: `demo-day`
(`hackathon` when you have five minutes and a rubric with weights).

## Steps

1. **The question**: write the challenge as one how-might-we question (`hmw`): who, the constraint, what success looks like.
2. **Open loud**: `cover_poster` with the team and track; 10 seconds, no agenda slide.
3. **Solution**: one line and three benefits (`solution`), one real screenshot.
4. **Demo**: the user goal in three steps (`demo`); rehearse the live run twice and keep the screenshot as the backup.
5. **Proof**: three numbers with `source:` (`kpi`): speed, accuracy, users or time saved, each against a baseline.
6. **Links**: `links` with demo, code and slides; say it out loud and stop talking.
7. **Scaffold**: `new --event demo-day` (theme `aurora-mesh`; `synthwave` or `cobalt-pop` for a louder room).
8. **QA**: stopwatch run under three minutes; the demo works offline or has a recorded fallback; lint and previews.

## Traps

An agenda slide; explaining the architecture before showing the product; a live demo that depends on venue wifi; numbers
with no baseline; reading the slide aloud.

## Output

Deck source, `.pptx`, `.pdf`, a 60-second backup recording of the demo, a one-page answer sheet for likely questions
(`communication/judge-qa.md`).
