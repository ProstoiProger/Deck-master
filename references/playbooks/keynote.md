# Playbook: keynote (conference talk, 20 minutes or more)

**Preconditions**: a stage, a room of strangers, one idea worth remembering; fewer words per slide than any other deck.
Event: `keynote`.

## Steps

1. **The idea**: one sentence the audience should repeat a week later; everything else serves it.
2. **Open with a story**: `cover_poster`, then a `statement` that makes one fact impossible to ignore (sourced).
3. **Problem**: `problem` with one person who lived it.
4. **One number**: `big_number` that carries the argument; let it sit for a few seconds.
5. **The idea**: `solution`, then `demo` (90 seconds, rehearsed, with a recorded backup).
6. **A voice**: `quote` from a user or an expert (with permission), or `image_full` with a credit.
7. **Close**: one action for Monday on the `closing` slide.
8. **Scaffold**: `new --event keynote` (theme `ember`; `signal-yellow` or `cobalt-pop` for a younger room).
9. **QA**: at most 25 words on a slide; the notes carry the talk; the clicker run is timed; the font size reads from the back row.

## Traps

An agenda slide; slides that repeat the speaker; a demo that depends on the venue network; ending on "Questions?" instead of the action.

## Output

Deck source, `.pptx`, `.pdf`, speaker script with timings, a one-page handout if the organisers ask.
