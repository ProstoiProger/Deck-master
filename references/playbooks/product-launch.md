# Playbook: product launch

**Preconditions**: a product or release with a clear promise; an audience of customers, press or partners; 8 to 12 minutes.
Event: `product-launch`.

## Steps

1. **The promise**: one sentence the audience can repeat; write it before any slide.
2. **Hook**: `cover_poster`, then a `statement` with the number that makes the pain real (sourced).
3. **Problem and solution**: `problem` with one user story, `solution` with one screenshot, `feature_grid` with four capabilities.
4. **Who it is for**: `persona` built from real interviews, not a stock photo person.
5. **Show it**: `demo` in three steps; a backup screenshot.
6. **Price**: `pricing` with one highlighted plan and the reason.
7. **Proof**: `proof_wall` with early users, pilots, awards or press, each with a caption that says where it comes from.
8. **Plan**: `roadmap` with dates for the first 90 days; `closing` with one call to action.
9. **Scaffold**: `new --event product-launch` (theme `sunset-mesh`; `signal-yellow` or `coral-market` for a business audience).
10. **QA**: the promise appears on slide 1 and on the last slide; claims are backed by proof; lint, previews, timed run.

## Traps

A feature tour with no user; superlatives with no proof; a roadmap with no dates; a price with no currency or period.

## Output

Deck source, `.pptx`, `.pdf`, speaker script, a one-page press summary if asked.
