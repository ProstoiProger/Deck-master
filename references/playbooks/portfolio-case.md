# Playbook: portfolio and case study

**Preconditions**: you have finished work you may show; 8 to 15 minutes or a leave-behind PDF.

## Steps

1. **Audience**: hiring manager, client or committee; pick the two projects that answer their question.
2. **Who**: one `profile` slide: role, base, experience, a one-line promise, contact.
3. **Skills and path**: `skills` and `timeline`; keep both to one slide each.
4. **Case block per project**: `case_header` (role, year, client, tools), `problem` (who struggled, one number),
   `process` (steps, your part), `gallery` (the work), `before_after` (the change), `kpi` or `quote` (the result).
5. **Images**: collect them first; crop consistently; label placeholders `illustrative`; check permission to show each one.
6. **Scaffold**: `new --event portfolio` (theme `portfolio-mono`) or `case-study` (`portfolio-warm`); `editorial` for a
   magazine feel.
7. **QA**: every image path resolves; results have a source or are described as observed; lint, previews.

## Traps

Showing outputs without the problem; "we" instead of "I" on the role; screens too small to read; no result; confidential
client data on a slide.

## Output

Deck source, `.pptx`, `.pdf` (the usual leave-behind), notes if you present live.
