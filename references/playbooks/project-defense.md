# Playbook: project defense

**Preconditions**: a finished project or thesis; a committee asks questions; 10 to 20 minutes plus Q&A.

## Steps

1. **What was asked**: the task or research question in the committee's words.
2. **What was done**: method in one or two slides; only what is needed to read the results.
3. **What was proved**: the main result with its evidence and uncertainty; the baseline it beats.
4. **What remains**: limits and open questions, stated first.
5. **Appendix for questions**: methods details, extra experiments, data description, ethics; each as a slide the
   speaker can jump to.
6. **Scaffold**: `new --event research-talk` and adjust minutes; theme `graphite-cyan` or `cloud-light`.
7. **Q&A prep**: write the 10 hardest questions and answers with `communication/judge-qa.md`; link each to an
   appendix slide.
8. **QA**: lint, previews, trace the committee's criteria if given, timed rehearsal.

## Examples

| Situation | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| committee reads the deck beforehand | PDF with more text on slides and fewer notes |
| strict time | cut background; keep the claim, evidence, limits |

## Traps

Defending effort instead of results; equations without meaning; no appendix, so every question is a surprise.

## Output

Deck source, `.pptx`, `.pdf`, Q&A prep with backup slides, timing table.
