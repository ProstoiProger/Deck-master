# Domain card template

A domain card tells the agent what a topic's audience expects on slides. Keep it to 10 to 20 lines. Copy this
structure into `domains/cards/<name>.md`, then add the name to `references/00-index.md` and the routing table in
`SKILL.md`.

## Structure

1. **Typical decks**: the events and audiences where this topic appears.
2. **What the audience asks first**: the three doubts to answer early.
3. **Evidence expected**: metrics, studies, pilots, certifications.
4. **Vocabulary**: terms to use and terms to avoid.
5. **Slides that matter**: layouts and the order.
6. **Risks and compliance**: privacy, safety, regulation, claims not to make.
7. **Typical visuals**: screenshots, diagrams, charts that fit.
8. **Sources to check**: where numbers and facts come from; mark facts that change with a "verified on" date.

## Rules

- Facts that change over time carry a "verified on" date and a source.
- Regulatory or legal statements say "verify the applicable rules for your country".
- No real people, companies or customer data in examples.
