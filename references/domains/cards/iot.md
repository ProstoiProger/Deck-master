# Domain card: IoT and embedded systems

- **Typical decks**: hardware hackathons, smart-city and energy cases, engineering courses.
- **Audience asks first**: does it work on real hardware, what does it cost, how reliable is it, how is it maintained.
- **Evidence expected**: a photo or recording of the working device, measurements with units and sample size, power
  and cost figures, failure and recovery behaviour.
- **Vocabulary**: sensors, actuators, gateway, edge, latency, battery life; units always.
- **Slides that matter**: `solution` (device photo), `architecture` (device, gateway, cloud, with the key part
  highlighted), `pipeline` (data from sensor to decision), `demo` (recording as backup), `kpi` (accuracy, latency,
  power), `roadmap`.
- **Risks**: device security and updates, privacy of sensor data, safety of physical actuation, regulatory marking.
- **Visuals**: your own photos, block diagrams, time-series charts (`chart: line`).
- **Sources**: lab measurements in a result file; datasheets with revision dates.
