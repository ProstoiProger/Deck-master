# Domain card: fintech and banking

Regulation and market facts change; verify the applicable rules for your country and mark facts with a date.

- **Typical decks**: bank hackathons with ML, AI and DS tracks, credit and fraud cases, payments products.
- **Audience asks first**: what is the monetary value, is it explainable to a regulator, is the data protected,
  how does it fit existing systems.
- **Evidence expected**: out-of-time validation, a business metric (loss avoided, approval rate at equal risk,
  detection at a fixed review capacity), stability across segments, fairness and explainability notes.
- **Vocabulary**: risk, loss, approval rate, false positive cost, customer impact; avoid unexplained ML terms.
- **Slides that matter**: `problem` with a money number, `pipeline` (data and validation), `kpi` (value at
  constraint), `comparison` (against current rules), `impact` (limits, compliance).
- **Risks**: personal and transaction data (aggregates only, `ops/security.md`), fair lending, model risk
  management, explainability of adverse decisions.
- **Visuals**: lift and cost curves, decision-threshold charts, layered architecture.
- **Sources**: the case brief, your validation log, public regulator pages with dates.
