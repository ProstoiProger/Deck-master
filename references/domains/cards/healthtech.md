# Domain card: health technology

Medical claims and regulation are sensitive; verify the applicable rules for your country and avoid clinical claims
you cannot support.

- **Typical decks**: health hackathons, course projects, research talks.
- **Audience asks first**: is it safe, who is accountable, what data was used and was consent given.
- **Evidence expected**: validation on data separate from development, clinical or user benefit stated carefully,
  error analysis for the harmful mistakes, comparison with the current practice.
- **Vocabulary**: use clinicians' terms for outcomes; say "decision support", not "diagnosis", unless validated and approved.
- **Slides that matter**: `problem` (burden), `solution`, `kpi` (sensitivity and specificity with intervals),
  `impact` (limits and safety), `architecture` (where data stays).
- **Risks**: patient privacy (no identifiable data, synthetic or consented only), bias across groups,
  over-claiming, regulatory status.
- **Visuals**: simple charts with intervals; avoid alarming imagery.
- **Sources**: peer-reviewed studies and guidelines with publication dates.
