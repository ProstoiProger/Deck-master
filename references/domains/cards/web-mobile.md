# Domain card: web and mobile products

- **Typical decks**: product hackathons, startup demo days, course projects.
- **Audience asks first**: who is the user, does it work, why would they come back.
- **Evidence expected**: usability test with a few users, task completion time, a working build, retention or sign-up
  numbers only if real.
- **Vocabulary**: user tasks and outcomes ("book a room in 3 taps"), not frameworks.
- **Slides that matter**: `problem` with the user, `solution` with a screenshot, `demo` (callouts match the live
  steps), `architecture` (client, API, data), `roadmap`.
- **Risks**: accessibility, privacy and consent, app store or platform rules, unlicensed UI kits and icons.
- **Visuals**: real screenshots in device frames, one flow per slide.
- **Sources**: your test notes and analytics export; never invented traction.
