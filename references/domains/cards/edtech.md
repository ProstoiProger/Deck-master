# Domain card: education technology

- **Typical decks**: education hackathons, course projects, pilot reports. The `classic-craft` theme fits game-based
  learning.
- **Audience asks first**: does it improve learning, is it fun without being a distraction, does it fit a teacher's week.
- **Evidence expected**: a small pilot with a comparison group, completion and retention, teacher feedback, privacy
  of minors handled.
- **Vocabulary**: learning goals, practice, feedback; avoid "gamification" as the only claim.
- **Slides that matter**: `problem` (drop-off number), `feature_grid` (mechanics), `demo`, `kpi` (completion against
  baseline), `roadmap` (pilot).
- **Risks**: data of minors and consent, accessibility, claims about grades without a control group.
- **Visuals**: screenshots with synthetic student data; progress bars; the game themes for playful tone (characters: `design/entities.md`).
- **Sources**: your pilot export and survey, with sample sizes stated.
