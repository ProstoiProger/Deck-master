# Domain card: cybersecurity

- **Typical decks**: security hackathons, CTF write-ups, risk reviews, course assignments.
- **Audience asks first**: what is the threat, how do you know, what is the residual risk, is this legal and authorised.
- **Evidence expected**: a threat model, reproduction steps in a safe lab, detection rates with false positives,
  mapping to a known framework where relevant.
- **Vocabulary**: assets, threats, controls, impact; avoid exploit detail that is not needed for the decision.
- **Slides that matter**: `problem` (threat and impact), `architecture` (attack path or defence layers),
  `demo` (in a lab with synthetic targets), `comparison` (controls and cost), `impact` (residual risk).
- **Risks**: never show real credentials, real hosts or live exploit steps against systems you do not own; state the
  authorisation for any testing; disclose responsibly.
- **Visuals**: layered defence diagrams, attack-chain pipelines, `terminal-slate` theme.
- **Sources**: advisories and standards with their publication dates.
