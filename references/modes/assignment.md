# Mode: assignment

A coursework or assignment presentation is marked against a handout. The deck must make every marking line visible
and show that the work is the presenter's own.

## Approach

1. Copy every marking criterion into the front matter `rubric:` in the handout's order, with weights.
2. Make the agenda slide the rubric lines in plain words; one section per criterion.
3. Give heavier criteria more slides and more time.
4. Show evidence for each line: a figure, a table, a code snippet, a result, a reference.
5. Close with what was achieved, the limits and what remains.
6. Put references and long tables in an appendix; say "see appendix" on the slide.

## Skeleton

Use `assets/templates/deck-assignment.md` (8 to 15 slides, 8 to 12 minutes): title, agenda, requirements, method
or design, main result, comparison or evaluation, conclusion and limits, closing, then appendix. Theme `cloud-light`
suits printing and bright rooms.

## Marker-friendly rules

- Use the handout's own terms in titles so the marker finds each criterion.
- Cite sources on the slide or in a references slide; follow the required citation style.
- Show your own work: screenshots of your run, your numbers, your decisions and why.
- Respect word, slide and time limits exactly; the cap is a requirement.
- Submit the PDF if the PPTX relies on fonts the marker may lack.

## Traps

- A chapter-by-chapter summary of the report instead of the rubric as the structure.
- Copying figures without attribution.
- No limits section, so the marker supplies the criticism.
- Reading slides aloud: notes say something the slide does not.
